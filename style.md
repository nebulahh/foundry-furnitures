# Style Guide

## Brand
FurniLux is a considered furniture and interior shop for people who want a home to feel calm, lived-in, and quietly distinctive. The experience should feel like a contemporary interiors journal with the utility of a dependable shop.

## Visual Direction
- Lead with warm daylight, honest materials, and tactile product photography. Use the supplied reference for its clear hierarchy and category/product density, not as a literal page layout.
- Palette: chalk `#F6F4EF`, ink `#252922`, forest `#354B38`, moss `#78836A`, muted clay `#B97858`, and soft line `#E2E0D9`. Reserve forest for primary actions and selected states.
- Typography: use an expressive editorial serif for display headlines and a crisp, legible sans serif for navigation, prices, controls, and body copy. Keep type compact in commerce controls and generous in editorial moments.
- Photography: real-looking interior and product imagery with natural light, visible grain/material, and varied room contexts. Avoid gradients, generic illustration, and product tiles that feel like admin cards.
- Layout: restrained top navigation, broad editorial hero, clear category entry points, product grids with ample image area, and a compact footer. Keep shopping information scannable.

## Interaction
- Navigation and filters should be predictable and keyboard accessible.
- Product cards expose price, material/variant context, and a clear add action without cluttering the image.
- The cart is persistent, has an unmistakable item count, and gives immediate feedback when changed.
- Checkout is a focused, reassuring form with a visible order summary and explicit delivery/contact fields.
- Use restrained transitions for menus, cart feedback, and page entrance. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Responsive Behavior
- Design mobile-first. Collapse navigation into a usable menu, keep cart access visible, reduce product grid columns, and stack checkout form and summary.
- Prevent horizontal overflow. Maintain comfortable tap targets and do not hide essential price or action information on small screens.

## Content Voice
Warm, specific, and calm. Describe tangible qualities such as solid oak, linen, joinery, comfort, and provenance. Avoid inflated claims, fake scarcity, and empty luxury language.
